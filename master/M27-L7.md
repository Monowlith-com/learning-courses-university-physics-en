# M27-L7 – Open quantum systems and the Lindblad equation {#L7}

**Course area:** M27 – Master: Advanced quantum theory

**Level:** Master · advanced specialization

**Prerequisites:** M27-L4: density operator; M13: statistical ensembles. [Previous lesson](M27-L6.md).

**Learning objective:** Apply the GKSL form with a consistent rate convention and calculate population decay versus coherence decay.

## Guiding question and context {#S1}

A system exchanges energy and information with its environment. After tracing out environmental degrees of freedom, its density operator often evolves nonunitarily. Under the standard finite-dimensional assumptions, a time-homogeneous, Markovian and completely positive dynamical semigroup admits Lindblad form.

## Physical model and derivation {#S2}

For a dimensionless jump operator $L$ and nonnegative rate $\gamma$, the dissipator is $\gamma(L\rho L^\dagger-\{L^\dagger L,\rho\}/2)$. Choose $L=|g\rangle\langle e|$ and $H=0$ (or the interaction picture for a diagonal Hamiltonian). Then $\dot\rho_{ee}=-\gamma\rho_{ee}$ while $\dot\rho_{eg}=-\gamma\rho_{eg}/2$. Excited population decays as $e^{-\gamma t}$ and any initial coherence as $e^{-\gamma t/2}$. In the alternative convention $L\to\sqrt\gamma L$, the external rate factor must be removed.

$$
\dot\rho=-\frac{i}{\hbar}[H,\rho]+\gamma\left(L\rho L^\dagger-\tfrac12\{L^\dagger L,\rho\}\right),\qquad L=|g\rangle\langle e|.
$$

## Worked example and teaching figure {#S3}

Starting from $|e\rangle$ with $\gamma=2\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, at $t=(\ln2)/\gamma\approx0.347\,\mathrm s$ the excited population is $\rho_{ee}=1/2$ and the ground population is $1/2$. If an initial coherence were present, its magnitude at the same time would be multiplied by $e^{-\gamma t/2}=1/\sqrt2\approx0.707$. The figure compares both rates. Emission from an initially fully excited state does not itself create coherence.

![Decay of excited-state population and more slowly decaying coherence amplitude in Lindblad dynamics.](../../../../media/course/U_PHY_153_EN.svg)

*Under pure amplitude damping, coherence decays at half the population rate.*

## Self-check {#S4}

### Exercises

1. What constraints apply to $\gamma$ in this GKSL convention?
2. Find $\rho_{ee}$ at $\gamma t=1$ when $\rho_{ee}(0)=1$.
3. What changes if $\sqrt\gamma$ is already included in $L$?

### Solutions

1. $\gamma\ge0$ with units of inverse time if $L$ is dimensionless.
2. $e^{-1}\approx0.368$.
3. The explicit external factor $\gamma$ multiplying the dissipator is omitted.

## Limits of validity and quality {#S5}

Time-independent GKSL dynamics assumes a suitable Markovian semigroup description. Memory effects, strong system-bath coupling and time-dependent rates may require different models. The jump operator does not by itself imply an actually observed event without an accompanying measurement model.
