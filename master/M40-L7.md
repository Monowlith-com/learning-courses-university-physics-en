# M40-L7 – Acceptance, Commissioning and Uncertainty Budgets in Medical Physics {#L7}

**Course area:** M40 – Master: Advanced Medical Physics and Quality Assurance

**Level:** Master · advanced specialization

**Prerequisites:** M32: measurement uncertainty and experimental design; M40-L1–L6.

**Learning objective:** Distinguish acceptance testing, commissioning characterization and routine control, and combine simple uncertainties.

## Guiding question and context {#S1}

Medical-physics systems can produce correct individual measurements yet fail under untested operating conditions. Initial acceptance, detailed pre-use characterization and continuing quality control are therefore distinct documented steps. Safety-critical uses require traceable deviations and required independent checks.

## Physical model and derivation {#S2}

For $n$ independent uncorrelated relative standard uncertainties $u_i$, sufficiently linear error propagation permits $u_c=\sqrt{\sum_i u_i^2}$. An expanded interval $U=k u_c$ with illustrative coverage factor $k=2$ is not automatically a safety or acceptance threshold. Correlations require covariance terms; systematic biases require separate correction or assessment. Test limits come from applicable procedures, not this classroom calculation.

$$
u_c=\sqrt{u_1^2+u_2^2+u_3^2},\qquad U=k\,u_c.
$$

## Worked example and teaching figure {#S3}

With illustrative relative standard uncertainties $u_1=0.8\%$, $u_2=1.2\%$ and $u_3=0.6\%$, the combination is $u_c=\sqrt{0.64+1.44+0.36}\%\approx1.56\%$. Formally, $U\approx3.12\%$ at $k=2$. The figure distinguishes acceptance, commissioning and recurrent checks; the numbers are not real-world tolerances.

## Self-check {#S4}

### Exercises

1. What is $u_c$ using only uncorrelated $u_1$ and $u_2$?
2. Why does passing acceptance not eliminate the need for recurring calibration checks?
3. What changes if uncertainty components are correlated?

### Solutions

1. $\sqrt{0.8^2+1.2^2}\%\approx1.44\%$.
2. System states and instruments may drift; ongoing checks detect later changes.
3. Additional covariance terms can increase or decrease the combined uncertainty.

## Limits of validity and quality {#S5}

Without a specified measurement protocol, traceability, correlation model and documented acceptance criteria, the analysis cannot authorize a system for service. It does not replace qualification, regulatory requirements or independent clinical quality assurance.

## Summary and outlook {#S6}
M40 closes the advanced medical-physics sequence from measurement data to verifiable application.
