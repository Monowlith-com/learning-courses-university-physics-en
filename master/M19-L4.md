# M19-L4 – Weighted Regression and Model Criticism {#L4}

**Course area:** M19 – Bachelor: Advanced Experimental Methods and Research Practice

**Level:** Bachelor

**Prerequisites:** M16: regression; M17: computation; M18: measurement reports. [Previous lesson](M19-L3.md).

**Learning objective:** Evaluate a weighted fit with residuals and degrees of freedom, and assess model assumptions.

## Question and Model {#S1}

Regression does not automatically validate a physical model. It estimates parameters inside a chosen function family under a stipulated error model. For $y_i=a+bx_i+\epsilon_i$, specify the units and whether uncertainty in $x_i$ can be neglected. Calibration reference values can induce correlations. Maximising $R^2$ does not replace a residual study or a physical explanation.

## Equations and Assumptions {#S2}

With uncorrelated observations and known standard uncertainties $u_i$, a common objective is
$$
\chi^2(a,b)=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\frac{[y_i-(a+bx_i)]^2}{u_i^2}.
$$
A two-parameter fit to $N$ independent points ordinarily has $\nu=N-2$ residual degrees of freedom. However, $\chi^2/\nu$ is not a universal pass/fail score: estimated, correlated or non-Gaussian errors alter the interpretation and reference distribution. Assumptions must be checked before applying thresholds.

## Worked Example and Diagram {#S3}

Use three synthetic readings $(x,y)=(0,1.1),(1,3.0),(2,5.1)$ each with $u_y=0.1$. Identical weights yield a least-squares line $\hat y\approx1.0667+2x$. Residuals are approximately $(+0.0333,-0.0667,+0.0333)$, hence $\chi^2\approx0.6667$ for $\nu=1$. Three points are too few to assess residual distributions reliably or generalise to arbitrary experiments. They serve as a transparent worked example. ## Self-assessment {#S4}

### Exercises

1. How many residual degrees of freedom does a two-parameter line fitted to three points have?
2. Calculate $\hat y$ at $x=1$ for $\hat y=1.0667+2x$.
3. Why does $R^2\approx1$ alone not validate a physical model?

### Solutions

1. It is $\nu=3-2=1$.
2. $\hat y\approx3.0667$.
3. An apparently excellent curve fit can conceal measurement errors, correlations or a wrong model family.

## Limits and Quality Control {#S5}

Alongside $\chi^2$, examine parameter units, treatment of outliers, temporal structure and dependence of residuals on $x$. A curved residual pattern suggests an inadequate model class, whereas random-looking residuals do not prove physical correctness. Nonlinear or correlated problems call for appropriate covariance models and numerical tests. Preserve raw data and the full fitting procedure for reproducibility.

## Summary and Outlook {#S6}

Parameters, uncertainty and residuals must be evaluated along with the assumptions of the model.
