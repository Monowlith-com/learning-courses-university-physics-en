# M30-L8 – Electroweak symmetry breaking and the Higgs mechanism {#L8}

**Course area:** M30 – Master: Quantum field theory and the Standard Model

**Level:** Master · advanced specialization

**Prerequisites:** M30-L7: gauge symmetries; M30-L5: effective potentials · [Previous lesson](M30-L7.md).

**Learning objective:** Distinguish a symmetric potential from a chosen vacuum configuration and estimate the W-boson mass.

## Guiding question and context {#S1}

Electromagnetism and the weak interaction form the electroweak sector of the Standard Model. Its local gauge description remains valid even though the Higgs field has a nonzero vacuum expectation value. The physical W and Z bosons consequently acquire masses, while the photon remains massless.

## Physical model and derivation {#S2}

A simplified real slice through a symmetric potential is $V(\varphi)=\lambda(\varphi^2-v^2)^2/4$ with $\lambda>0$. The potential is invariant under $\varphi\mapsto-\varphi$ but has minima at $\varphi=\pm v$. This illustrates the choice of a vacuum configuration, but is **not** the full gauge-invariant Higgs-doublet theory. In the Standard Model, the vacuum expectation value $v\approx246\,\mathrm{GeV}$ gives a mass term to the charged W boson through the Higgs-doublet kinetic term. Local gauge freedom is a redundancy of field variables, not an ordinary physical global symmetry literally broken by the vacuum.

$$
m_W=\frac{gv}{2}\qquad(\hbar=c=1).
$$

## Worked example and teaching figure {#S3}

Using $g\approx0.65$ and $v\approx246\,\mathrm{GeV}$ gives $m_W\approx0.65\times246/2\approx79.95\,\mathrm{GeV}$. The sketch of a double-well potential shows a **real toy-model slice** with minima at $\pm v$, not the topology of the full electroweak Higgs-field space.

![Symmetric double-well potential as a simplified real slice with two minima at minus v and plus v.](../../../../media/course/U_PHY_131_EN.svg)

*Symmetric dynamics can allow several equivalent candidate vacuum configurations.*

## Self-check {#S4}

### Exercises

1. Where are the minima of $V(\varphi)=\lambda(\varphi^2-v^2)^2/4$?
2. Evaluate $gv/2$ with $g=0.65$ and $v=246$.
3. Is the drawn double well the full Higgs-doublet potential?

### Solutions

1. At $\varphi=-v$ and $\varphi=+v$.
2. Approximately $79.95\,\mathrm{GeV}$.
3. No, only a simplified real slice illustrating vacuum selection.

## Limits of validity and quality {#S5}

The chosen value of $g$ is a rough scale-specific approximation; precision predictions include radiative corrections and a specified renormalization scheme. The double well is only an analogy, not a plot of the full gauge-orbit structure.
