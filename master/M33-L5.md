# M33-L5 – Laser Cooling: Doppler Force and Temperature Limit {#L5}

**Course area:** M33 – Master: Atomic, Molecular and Optical Physics

**Level:** Master · advanced specialization

**Prerequisites:** M1: momentum and energy; M33-L2: two-level system; M33-L4: photons.

**Learning objective:** Calculate the Doppler temperature from the natural linewidth under explicit model assumptions.

## Guiding question and context {#S1}

In Doppler cooling, red-detuned beams are arranged so that a moving atom preferentially absorbs photons counterpropagating to its motion. Averaged over many events, photon recoil slows the atom. Spontaneous emission simultaneously produces random recoil and momentum diffusion. The balance between friction and diffusion creates a limiting temperature in the simplest model.

## Physical model and derivation {#S2}

For a closed two-level transition with spontaneous decay rate $\Gamma$ (angular-frequency linewidth in $\mathrm{s}^{-1}$), weak saturation, and optimal red detuning $\Delta=-\Gamma/2$, the elementary Doppler model gives $T_D=\hbar\Gamma/(2k_B)$. Here $\Gamma$ is the angular-frequency linewidth; a quoted linewidth $\Gamma/(2\pi)$ in hertz must be multiplied by $2\pi$ before substitution. This is not an absolute bound on all cooling methods.

$$
T_D=\frac{\hbar\Gamma}{2k_B}\qquad\left(\Delta=-\frac{\Gamma}{2}\right).
$$

## Worked example and teaching figure {#S3}

For a modeled transition with $\Gamma/(2\pi)=6.0\,\mathrm{MHz}$, $\Gamma=2\pi\cdot6.0\cdot10^6\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$. With $\hbar=1.054571817\cdot10^{-34}\,\mathrm{J\,s}$ and $k_B=1.380649\cdot10^{-23}\,\mathrm{J/K}$, we obtain $T_D\approx1.44\cdot10^{-4}\,\mathrm K=144\,\mathrm{\mu K}$. The diagram sketches counterpropagating beams and the viscous force.

*Doppler cooling balances velocity-dependent scattering against recoil diffusion.*

## Self-check {#S4}

### Exercises

1. What happens to $T_D$ if $\Gamma$ doubles?
2. What is the sign of the cooling-beam detuning?
3. Why is it possible to cool below $T_D$?

### Solutions

1. It doubles in the same model.
2. $\Delta<0$ (red detuning).
3. Additional internal structure and polarization gradients enable other mechanisms; further stages can also follow.

## Limits of validity and quality {#S5}

The equation assumes low intensity, an effectively closed two-level transition, and appropriate opposing beams. Multilevel atoms, sub-Doppler processes, finite recoil energy, and magneto-optical traps require richer models. The linewidth here is a hypothetical example, not a measured species parameter.
