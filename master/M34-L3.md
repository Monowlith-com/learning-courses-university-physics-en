# M34-L3 – Quantum Gates, Circuits and Bell-State Preparation {#L3}

**Course area:** M34 – Master: Quantum Information and Quantum Technologies

**Level:** Master · advanced specialization

**Prerequisites:** M34-L1: qubit amplitudes; M34-L2: Bell states.

**Learning objective:** Derive a Bell state from |00⟩ using H and CNOT and calculate measurement correlations.

## Guiding question and context {#S1}

Quantum circuits separate reversible unitary operations from measurements. A Hadamard gate H creates a superposition from |0⟩. A controlled-NOT (CNOT) flips its target when the control is in basis state |1⟩. Together the two operations transform an initially separable state into an entangled one.

## Physical model and derivation {#S2}

Start in $|00\rangle$. Applying $(H\otimes I)$ yields $(|00\rangle+|10\rangle)/\sqrt2$. The subsequent $\mathrm{CNOT}$ converts the second branch into $|11\rangle$. Computational-basis outcomes are 00 and 11, each with probability 1/2. That distribution alone does not distinguish coherent entanglement from a classical mixture; other measurement bases are needed.

$$
\mathrm{CNOT}(H\otimes I)|00\rangle=\frac{|00\rangle+|11\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

The figure shows two input wires, a Hadamard gate on the upper wire and a subsequent CNOT controlled by that wire. For 200 ideal measurements, approximately 100 outcomes 00 and 100 outcomes 11 would be expected. Outcomes 01 and 10 do not occur ideally.

## Self-check {#S4}

### Exercises

1. What is the state immediately after H acts on the first qubit?
2. What is the ideal probability of 01 at the circuit output?
3. How can one distinguish the coherent state from a classical 00/11 mixture?

### Solutions

1. $(|00\rangle+|10\rangle)/\sqrt2$.
2. $0$.
3. By using correlations measured in an additional suitable basis, such as the X basis.

## Limits of validity and quality {#S5}

The circuit idealizes perfect qubits and gates. Real hardware has preparation, gate and readout errors. Gates may be physically implemented through different pulse sequences or interactions.
