# M41-L7 – Photonic Crystals, Periodic Index and Bragg Band Gaps {#L7}

**Course area:** M41 – Master: Photonics and Ultrafast Optics

**Level:** Master · advanced specialization

**Prerequisites:** M41-L1: modes; M41-L2: resonance; M35: Bloch states.

**Learning objective:** Estimate a simplified Bragg wavelength from period and effective index.

## Guiding question and context {#S1}

A photonic crystal has a spatially periodic dielectric structure. Along a periodic direction the fields admit Bloch-mode descriptions. Near Brillouin-zone boundaries, the periodic modulation can couple counterpropagating waves. With sufficiently effective index contrast, regions of reduced or forbidden propagation may emerge.

## Physical model and derivation {#S2}

For a simple weakly modulated one-dimensional Bragg structure, the center of first-order backscattering occurs approximately at $2\beta\approx2\pi/a$. Using $\beta\approx2\pi n_{\mathrm{eff}}/\lambda$ gives $\lambda_B\approx2n_{\mathrm{eff}}a$. The formula estimates a Bragg-resonance center, not an actual band-gap width or even proof that a full photonic band gap exists. Those depend on contrast, polarization, geometry and modal coupling.

$$
2\beta\simeq\frac{2\pi}{a}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\lambda_B\simeq2n_{\mathrm{eff}}a.
$$

## Worked example and teaching figure {#S3}

For $a=250\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $n_{\mathrm{eff}}=1.50$, $\lambda_B\approx750\,\mathrm{nm}$. If the period increases to $300\,\mathrm{nm}$ at the same assumed effective index, the estimate becomes $900\,\mathrm{nm}$. The figure connects real-space periodicity with schematic dispersion splitting near a zone boundary.

## Self-check {#S4}

### Exercises

1. Find $\lambda_B$ for $a=200\,\mathrm{nm}$ and $n_{\mathrm{eff}}=1.5$.
2. Which physical parameters affect band-gap width?
3. Why does the Bragg wavelength alone not establish a complete three-dimensional gap?

### Solutions

1. $600\,\mathrm{nm}$.
2. For instance index contrast, geometry, polarization and spatial mode coupling.
3. It considers only one idealized periodic direction and a simplified pair of modes.

## Limits of validity and quality {#S5}

Strong modulation, multidimensional crystals, anisotropic media and dispersion require complete Maxwell eigenvalue calculations. This illustration does not establish a band gap or replace optical transmission measurements.
