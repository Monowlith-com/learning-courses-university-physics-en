# M28-L3 – Landau–Ginzburg theory and order parameters {#L3}

**Course area:** M28 – Master: Nonequilibrium and many-body physics

**Level:** Master · advanced specialization

**Prerequisites:** M13: phase transitions; M26: field functionals · [Previous lesson](M28-L2.md).

**Learning objective:** Find stationary phases of a symmetric $\varphi^4$ model and explain the role of gradients.

## Guiding question and context {#S1}

Near a phase transition, many-body physics can often be characterized by an order parameter $\varphi$. In a ferromagnet it may describe magnetization along a selected direction. The two signs of this parameter distinguish possible ordered phases.

## Physical model and derivation {#S2}

If the system is invariant under $\varphi\mapsto-\varphi$ without an external field, the local free energy contains only even powers. Positive $b$ keeps the potential bounded below. Stationary values obey $\partial f/\partial\varphi=a\varphi+b\varphi^3-h=0$. For $h=0$ and $a>0$, the minimum lies at zero. For $a<0$, zero becomes unstable and minima occur at $\varphi_\pm=\pm\sqrt{-a/b}$. With $K>0$, the gradient term assigns an energy cost to spatial changes. Hence an interface between regions of opposite order parameter costs energy. This functional form is the starting point for fluctuation theory.

$$
F[\varphi]=\int\!\mathrm d^dx\,\left[\frac{K}{2}|\nabla\varphi|^2+\frac{a}{2}\varphi^2+\frac{b}{4}\varphi^4-h\varphi\right],\qquad \varphi_\pm=\pm\sqrt{-a/b}\ (a<0,h=0).
$$

## Worked example and teaching figure {#S3}

Choose dimensionless parameters $a=-2$ and $b=2$. The two minima lie at $\varphi=\pm1$. The second derivative $f^{\prime\prime}=a+3b\varphi^2$ is $4>0$ there, but $-2<0$ at the origin. The double-well graphic shows that a stationary-point condition alone does not establish stability.

![Symmetric double-well potential with stable minima at minus one and plus one and an unstable point at the origin.](../../../../media/course/U_PHY_141_EN.svg)

*A negative quadratic coefficient produces two symmetry-related ordered states.*

## Self-check {#S4}

### Exercises

1. Find all stationary values of $\varphi$ for $h=0$.
2. Where are the minima for $a=-8$, $b=2$?
3. What is the physical role of $K|\nabla\varphi|^2/2$?

### Solutions

1. $\varphi=0$ and, for $a<0$, also $\varphi=\pm\sqrt{-a/b}$.
2. $\varphi=\pm2$.
3. It penalizes rapid spatial variation and interfaces.

## Limits of validity and quality {#S5}

Landau theory is a mean-field approximation. Near critical points, fluctuations can modify critical exponents and even invalidate a simple expansion. Numerical values in the example are dimensionless.
