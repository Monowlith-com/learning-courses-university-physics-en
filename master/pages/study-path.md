# University – Physics – Study path {#studienweg}

This is a **didactic self-study suggestion**, not an official university semester plan, examination regulation or statement of mandatory modules. The provisional author plan contains 25 bachelor-oriented and 18 master-oriented modules, totalling 323 lessons.

## Starting point: concepts, mathematics and classical foundations {#S1}

Begin with [M1-L1](../M1-L1.md), review units and foundational mathematics, then move through [M2-L1](../M2-L1.md) to [M3-L1](../M3-L1.md). Next, [M4-L1](../M4-L1.md) through [M7-L1](../M7-L1.md) connect motion, waves, thermodynamics, fields and optics.

## Bachelor: theoretical and methodological development {#S2}

A connected theoretical sequence is [M8-L1](../M8-L1.md) → [M9-L1](../M9-L1.md) → [M10-L1](../M10-L1.md). Then [M11-L1](../M11-L1.md), [M12-L1](../M12-L1.md), [M13-L1](../M13-L1.md), [M14-L1](../M14-L1.md) and [M15-L1](../M15-L1.md) introduce quantum, statistical, solid-state and nuclear physics.

## Laboratory, computation and research {#S3}

For practical work, combine [M16-L1](../M16-L1.md) on data analysis, [M17-L1](../M17-L1.md) on simulations, [M18-L1](../M18-L1.md) on laboratory methods and [M19-L1](../M19-L1.md) on advanced measurement practice. Check dimensions, assumptions and uncertainty in each task; experiments require appropriate supervision and safety procedures.

## Bachelor elective fields {#S4}

After the foundations, you may focus on [M20-L1](../M20-L1.md) (astrophysics), [M21-L1](../M21-L1.md) (geophysics), [M22-L1](../M22-L1.md) (biophysics), [M23-L1](../M23-L1.md) (applied physics), [M24-L1](../M24-L1.md) (medical physics) and [M25-L1](../M25-L1.md) (interdisciplinary research practice). These are not presented as universally mandatory degree requirements.

## Master: methods and possible specializations {#S5}

Before studying advanced subjects, revise the relevant bachelor-level models and use [M26-L1](../M26-L1.md) for additional mathematical methods if needed. The routes below group related material for self-study; they do not claim formal admission prerequisites.

- **Quantum physics:** [M11](../M11-L1.md) → [M12](../M12-L1.md) → [M13](../M13-L1.md) → [M26](../M26-L1.md) → [M27](../M27-L1.md) → [M28](../M28-L1.md) → [M30](../M30-L1.md) → [M33](../M33-L1.md) → [M34](../M34-L1.md).
- **Materials and solid-state physics:** [M14](../M14-L1.md) → [M17](../M17-L1.md) → [M26](../M26-L1.md) → [M31](../M31-L1.md) → [M35](../M35-L1.md).

## Study routine and self-assessment {#S6}

For each lesson: identify its objectives, write down modelling assumptions, recalculate worked examples with units, attempt exercises before reading the solutions and assess the model’s domain of validity. Return to linked earlier lessons as needed. External links are supplementary; the [sources](sources.md) list addresses cited in the lesson drafts.
