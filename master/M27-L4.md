# M27-L4 – Density operators, mixed states and decoherence {#L4}

**Course area:** M27 – Master: Advanced quantum theory

**Level:** Master · advanced specialization

**Prerequisites:** M11: measurement postulates; M12: quantum evolution and operators. [Previous lesson](M27-L3.md).

**Learning objective:** Use density matrices for statistical ensembles, calculate purity and distinguish phase loss from population relaxation.

## Guiding question and context {#S1}

A pure-state vector is not sufficient when preparation is uncertain or when a subsystem of an entangled system is considered. The density operator represents quantum and statistical probabilities consistently; its diagonal and off-diagonal elements encode different information.

## Physical model and derivation {#S2}

For an ensemble $\{p_k,|\psi_k\rangle\}$ define $\rho=\sum_k p_k|\psi_k\rangle\langle\psi_k|$. Physical density matrices are Hermitian, positive semidefinite and trace-normalized. Observables obey $\langle A\rangle=\mathrm{tr}(\rho A)$. Consider an initial qubit state $\rho(0)=|+\rangle\langle+|$ with $|+\rangle=(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$. Under pure dephasing, the populations stay constant, while $\rho_{01}(t)=e^{-\Gamma t}\rho_{01}(0)$. The purity $\mathrm{tr}(\rho^2)$ then measures the loss of coherence.

$$
\rho(t)=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&e^{-\Gamma t}\\e^{-\Gamma t}&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad \mathrm{tr}[\rho(t)^2]=\frac{1+e^{-2\Gamma t}}{2}.
$$

## Worked example and teaching figure {#S3}

At $\Gamma t=\ln2$, the off-diagonal entries have decreased from $1/2$ to $1/4$. Purity is $(1+1/4)/2=5/8=0.625$, while the two populations remain $1/2$. The figure contrasts decaying coherence $2|\rho_{01}|$ and constant populations. Loss of interference is not the same as energy-population transfer.

![Two curves: constant state population and exponentially decaying qubit coherence.](../../../../media/course/U_PHY_235_EN.svg)

*Pure dephasing reduces coherence without changing populations.*

## Self-check {#S4}

### Exercises

1. What are the three defining conditions for physical density matrices?
2. Find the purity as $t\to\infty$ in this dephasing model.
3. Does pure dephasing change the diagonal entries in this example?

### Solutions

1. Hermiticity, positive semidefiniteness and unit trace.
2. Purity approaches $1/2$.
3. No. Both populations remain $1/2$.

## Limits of validity and quality {#S5}

Exponential decay reflects a Markovian or suitable effective dynamics, not every microscopic environment. A reduced density operator can be mixed even if the global state is pure. Purity alone is not a universal entanglement measure.
