# M37-L2 – Compact Objects, Accretion and Radiative Power {#L2}

**Course area:** M37 – Master: Astrophysics and Cosmology

**Level:** Master · advanced specialization

**Prerequisites:** M29: curved spacetime; M37-L1: stellar structure · [Previous lesson](M37-L1.md).

**Learning objective:** Distinguish accretion power from the Eddington limit and evaluate a simplified numerical example.

## Guiding question and context {#S1}

White dwarfs, neutron stars and black holes have different surfaces and gravitational environments. During accretion, infalling matter can convert gravitational energy to heat, radiation, outflows and motion. Observed luminosity depends not only on mass inflow but also on geometry, radiative transfer and energy losses.

## Physical model and derivation {#S2}

The simplified power relation is $L=\eta\dot M c^2$, with mass accretion rate $\dot M$ and radiative efficiency $\eta$. A separate concept is the Eddington luminosity $L_{\mathrm Edd}=4\pi GMm_pc/\sigma_T$ for fully ionized hydrogen, Thomson scattering and spherical isotropic emission. It follows by balancing radiation force and gravity per electron–proton pair; it is not a universal maximum for arbitrary accretion flows.

$$
L=\eta\dot Mc^2,\qquad L_{\mathrm{Edd}}=\frac{4\pi GMm_pc}{\sigma_T}.
$$

## Worked example and teaching figure {#S3}

For $\eta=0.10$ and $\dot M=10^{14}\,\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}$, the result is $L\approx8.99\times10^{29}\,\mathrm W$. The Eddington scale for a hypothetical ten-solar-mass object under pure-hydrogen assumptions is about $1.26\times10^{32}\,\mathrm W$. This is not a fit to a particular source.

## Self-check {#S4}

### Exercises

1. What happens to $L$ if $\dot M$ doubles at fixed $\eta$?
2. Find $\dot M$ for $L\approx9\times10^{28}\,\mathrm W$ and $\eta=0.1$.
3. Why can collimation or anisotropic flows lead to apparent luminosities above $L_{\mathrm{Edd}}$?

### Solutions

1. Luminosity doubles.
2. About $10^{13}\,\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}$.
3. The derivation assumes spherical isotropic radiation; collimated or optically thick flows violate that assumption.

## Limits of validity and quality {#S5}

The energy balance parametrizes efficiency, not detailed flow physics. The Eddington relation assumes Thomson opacity; composition, relativity, magnetic fields and disk geometry modify the comparison.
