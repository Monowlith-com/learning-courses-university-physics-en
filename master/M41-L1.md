# M41-L1 – Waveguides, Guided Modes and Effective Refractive Index {#L1}

**Course area:** M41 – Master: Photonics and Ultrafast Optics

**Level:** Master · advanced specialization

**Prerequisites:** M7: wave optics; M33: lasers and photons; M35: materials physics; M40 · [Previous lesson](M40-L7.md).

**Learning objective:** Compute a guided-mode effective index from the propagation constant and distinguish phase from group velocity.

## Guiding question and context {#S1}

An integrated optical waveguide confines light in a guided mode through a transverse refractive-index profile. Beyond ray sketches, one solves an electromagnetic boundary-value problem: field profiles have transverse modal structure and associated propagation constants. A high core index by itself does not guarantee single-mode operation.

## Physical model and derivation {#S2}

A locally monochromatic mode carries a longitudinal phase factor $\exp(i\beta z-i\omega t)$. With vacuum wavenumber $k_0=2\pi/\lambda_0$, define the effective phase index $n_{\mathrm{eff}}=\beta/k_0$. For a conventional lossless index-guided mode, $n_{\mathrm{clad}}<n_{\mathrm{eff}}<n_{\mathrm{core}}$. By contrast, the group index is $n_g=c\,d\beta/d\omega$ and need not equal $n_{\mathrm{eff}}$ in a dispersive guide.

$$
\beta=\frac{2\pi n_{\mathrm{eff}}}{\lambda_0},\qquad v_{\mathrm{ph}}=\frac{\omega}{\beta}=\frac{c}{n_{\mathrm{eff}}}.
$$

## Worked example and teaching figure {#S3}

For an illustrative mode at $\lambda_0=1.55\,\mathrm{\mu m}$ and $n_{\mathrm{eff}}=1.70$, one obtains $\beta=6.89\,\mathrm{\mu m^{-1}}$. With $n_{\mathrm{core}}=2.0$ and $n_{\mathrm{clad}}=1.45$, this lies inside the expected index range. The axial phase period is $\lambda_0/n_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx0.912\,\mathrm{\mu m}$. The figure gives a schematic modal field, not a numerical eigenmode solution.

## Self-check {#S4}

### Exercises

1. Compute $\beta$ for $n_{\mathrm{eff}}=1.50$ at the same vacuum wavelength.
2. How does phase velocity change as effective index rises?
3. Why cannot group index be determined from a single phase-index measurement?

### Solutions

1. $\beta=2\pi(1.50)/1.55\approx6.08\,\mathrm{\mu m^{-1}}$.
2. $v_{\mathrm{ph}}=c/n_{\mathrm{eff}}$ decreases.
3. The group index requires the frequency derivative of the propagation constant.

## Limits of validity and quality {#S5}

The index bounds refer to the simple lossless guiding model. Leaky modes, complex material constants, anisotropy, metamaterials and strong dispersion require more complete treatment.
