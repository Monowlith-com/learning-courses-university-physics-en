# Course introduction – University Physics {#kurseinstieg}

This self-study course moves from the foundations of physics to selected advanced research topics. It contains **43 modules and 323 lessons**, in both German and English. Its sequence is a **provisional editorial study plan**, not an official university degree or examination curriculum.

## Audience and prerequisites {#S1}

The course is intended for students and independent learners seeking a systematic introduction or a focused review of physics. School-level mathematics (algebra, functions and trigonometry) supports the initial modules. Advanced topics require calculus, linear algebra, differential equations and the physical models developed earlier in the sequence.

## Learning objectives and study method {#S2}

Learners should be able to explain physical models and their assumptions, derive and apply central relationships, evaluate measurements with units and uncertainty, and identify the limits of simulations and experiments. Lessons offer explanations, examples, self-check problems and source references. Work through problems before consulting the solutions.

## Course navigation {#S6}

For continuous study, start with [M1-L1](M1-L1.md) and work through the foundation modules in their listed sequence. Depending on prior knowledge, elective and graduate-level modules can be selected independently. The [study path](pages/study-path.md) explains thematic routes, and the [sources](pages/sources.md) gather external references.

## Scope and limitations {#S7}

The materials are original **author drafts**. Independent scientific, linguistic and technical review and final approval of the curriculum are still pending. They do not replace supervised laboratory work, safety instruction or an institution’s degree and assessment requirements.
