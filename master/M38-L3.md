# M38-L3 – Geodynamo, Magnetic Induction and Diffusion {#L3}

**Course area:** M38 – Master: Geophysics, Atmospheric Physics and Climate Models

**Level:** Master · advanced specialization

**Prerequisites:** M16: magnetic fields; M31: partial differential equations; M38-L2 · [Previous lesson](M38-L2.md).

**Learning objective:** Distinguish advection, field stretching and magnetic diffusion, and compute a magnetic Reynolds number for an illustrative model.

## Guiding question and context {#S1}

Much of Earth’s main magnetic field is produced by electric currents in its conducting liquid outer core. Thermal and chemical buoyancy drive flow, while rotation helps organize it. Dynamo theory asks under what conditions flow can sustain magnetic fields. One measured field map alone does not uniquely recover core flow.

## Physical model and derivation {#S2}

For an incompressible conducting fluid with constant magnetic diffusivity $\eta=1/(\mu_0\sigma)$, the induction equation is $\partial_t\mathbf B=\nabla\times(\mathbf u\times\mathbf B)+\eta\nabla^2\mathbf B$, subject to $\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0$. The first right-hand term describes transport and stretching; the second represents resistive diffusion. The dimensionless ratio $R_m=UL/\eta$ compares them at typical scales $U,L$. A large value alone does not prove self-sustained dynamo action.

$$
\frac{\partial\mathbf B}{\partial t}=\nabla\times(\mathbf u\times\mathbf B)+\eta\nabla^2\mathbf B,\qquad R_m=\frac{UL}{\eta}.
$$

## Worked example and teaching figure {#S3}

Using deliberately constructed scales $U=10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, $L=10^6\,\mathrm m$ and $\eta=2\,\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-1}}$, one finds $R_m=50$. The advective timescale is $L/U=10^{10}\,\mathrm s$, while diffusion takes $L^2/\eta=5\times10^{11}\,\mathrm s$. This does not predict the real terrestrial field strength.

## Self-check {#S4}

### Exercises

1. How does $R_m$ change if $U$ is halved?
2. What are the SI units of $\eta$?
3. Why does $R_m=50$ not automatically imply a dynamo instability?

### Solutions

1. It falls to 25.
2. $\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-1}}$.
3. It is a scaling ratio; geometry, boundaries, feedback and flow structure also matter.

## Limits of validity and quality {#S5}

The toy model assumes constant conductivity and scalar diffusivity. Real core flow is turbulent, multiscale and magnetohydrodynamically coupled. Reversals cannot be predicted from this single equation without flow and thermodynamic dynamics.
