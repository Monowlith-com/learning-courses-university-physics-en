# M36-L4 – Neutrino Oscillations and Mass Mixing {#L4}

**Course area:** M36 – Master: Particle Physics, Collision Experiments and Accelerators

**Level:** Master · advanced specialization

**Prerequisites:** M34: quantum superposition; M30: Standard Model.

**Learning objective:** Calculate an idealized two-flavour transition probability using consistent units.

## Guiding question and context {#S1}

Neutrino flavour states are superpositions of mass eigenstates. Their different propagation phases can change the measured flavour over a baseline. This is interference and evidence for nondegenerate neutrino masses. Real experiments generally require three flavours, matter effects and detector modelling.

## Physical model and derivation {#S2}

In vacuum and the two-flavour approximation, $P(\nu_\alpha\to\nu_\beta)=\sin^2(2\theta)\sin^2(\Delta m^2L/4E)$ in natural units. When $\Delta m^2$ is entered in $\mathrm{eV}^2$, $L$ in $\mathrm{km}$ and $E$ in $\mathrm{GeV}$, the phase becomes numerically $1.267\,\Delta m^2L/E$. The unit prescription is part of the formula; $1.267$ is not a fundamental dimensionless constant.

$$
P_{\alpha\to\beta}=\sin^2(2\theta)\,\sin^2\!\left(1.267\,\frac{\Delta m^2[\mathrm{eV}^2]L[\mathrm{km}]}{E[\mathrm{GeV}]}\right).
$$

## Worked example and teaching figure {#S3}

For maximal mixing $\theta=45^\circ$, $\Delta m^2=2.5\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{eV}^2$, $L=500\,\mathrm{km}$ and $E=1\,\mathrm{GeV}$, the phase is $1.58375$ and $P\approx0.9998$. The figure plots the ideal oscillation pattern against $L/E$, not a detector cross-section.

## Self-check {#S4}

### Exercises

1. What is $P$ for $\theta=0$?
2. How does the oscillation phase change when $E$ doubles at fixed $L$?
3. What length unit is required in the numerical $1.267$ formula?

### Solutions

1. $P=0$.
2. It is halved.
3. Kilometres.

## Limits of validity and quality {#S5}

The two-flavour vacuum model neglects three-flavour interference, the CP phase, matter effects and finite energy resolution. The parameters here serve as a calculation example, not a current global fit.
